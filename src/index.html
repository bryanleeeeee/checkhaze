PERSONAL GUIDANCE

Who should take extra care?

Children, older people, pregnant women and people with chronic heart or lung conditions can be more sensitive to haze. Choose “Children & sensitive” on the air quality screen to see the corresponding immediate-activity guidance.

If you feel unwell, reduce exposure and seek medical advice as needed. Your health and symptoms matter alongside the numbers.

Full NEA health advisory
HOW THIS APP WORKS

Independent app. Licensed data.

How precise is my location?

NEA publishes readings for North, South, East, West and Central Singapore. Phone location is matched to the nearest official regional reference point. Named places are approximate regional matches, not readings at an individual street.

How often does it update?

The app checks for new data every five minutes while open. The source feeds update about every 15 minutes, with readings representing hourly or rolling 24-hour periods. Each metric shows its own observation time. Delayed and saved readings are labelled.

Is my location stored?

Precise coordinates are used only on your device to select a reporting region. Your selected region, saved places, activity plans, watch settings, room sizing inputs and checklist stay in this browser. We do not send your coordinates to our server.

Privacy and your choices

CheckHaze’s application code uses precise coordinates only on this device to choose a reporting region. Coordinates are not sent to our API or stored by the app. Location is optional and requested only after you tap the location button; revoke permission in your browser settings.

Selected places, plans, health-profile preference, watch settings and room inputs are saved in this browser until you clear them below or clear site data. Avoid sensitive details in shared updates. Notifications are optional and work only while the app is open.

No advertising or analytics trackers are included in our app code. Hosting providers (ChatGPT Sites / Cloudflare) process requests and may retain IP addresses, browser information and operational logs under their own policies. We cannot verify their retention settings from the app. External sites apply their own privacy policies.

For general issues, use the GitHub issue tracker; do not post personal or health information publicly.
